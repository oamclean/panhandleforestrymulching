Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from Panhandle landowners.

What is forestry mulching?

A one-pass clearing method that uses a high-horsepower drum mulcher on a tracked carrier to grind brush, palmetto, and small trees into mulch in place — no burning, no hauling.

How much does it cost?

Most Panhandle properties come in $1,200–$2,800 per acre depending on density, terrain, and access.

How is mulching different from bush hogging?

A bush hog cuts grass and light brush — it chokes on anything woody. A forestry mulcher grinds standing brush, palmetto, and trees up to 8" diameter into mulch in a single pass.

Will it damage my soil or my trees?

No. Tracked carriers spread weight far better than dozers, and we mark every tree you want to keep before starting.

How long does it take?

Most jobs under five acres finish in 1–2 days.

Do you handle stumps?

Yes, on request. For pasture or recreational use, leaving stumps under mulch is often fine. For building pads we grind or pull.

Do you work in the rain?

Damp yes, saturated no — saturated ground rutes. We wait a few days after heavy rain.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We carry general liability and equipment insurance and can provide a certificate before work starts.

Where do you work?

The full Florida Panhandle — Pensacola to Tallahassee.
