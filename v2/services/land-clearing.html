Land Clearing

Full clearing for homesites, pasture, food plots, and commercial pads.

Need a piece of land turned from overgrowth to usable in a hurry? We handle the full clearing process — mulching, controlled removal of larger trees, optional stump grinding.

What's included

  • Mulching of all brush and small/medium trees
  • Selective removal of larger trees on request
  • Optional stump grinding and root rake
  • Final grade-ready surface when needed

Frequently asked

Do you pull stumps?

Yes, when needed. For pasture, leaving them under mulch is often fine. For build pads we grind or pull.

Can you clear in wet conditions?

Damp yes, saturated no — saturated ground damages soil and leaves ruts.

Do you handle permitting?

Permitting is the landowner's responsibility, but we provide whatever documentation a permit application needs.
