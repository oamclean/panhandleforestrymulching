Forestry Mulching

Grind standing brush in place — no burn pile, no hauling, no dozer scars.

Forestry mulching is the cleanest, fastest way to take overgrown acreage back. A high-horsepower mulcher head shreds standing growth in place, leaving a layer of mulch that protects your soil and slows future regrowth.

What's included

  • On-site grinding of brush, palmetto, briar, and trees up to 8" in diameter
  • Mulch left in place at 2–4 inches deep
  • Selective clearing — we mark trees you want to save
  • Daily progress photos and a final walkthrough

Frequently asked

How much does it cost in the Panhandle?

Most properties run $1,200–$2,800 per acre depending on density and access.

How long does the mulch layer last?

Typically 6–18 months, depending on rainfall and regrowth species.

Will it damage my soil?

No — tracked carriers spread weight, and the mulch layer reduces erosion.
