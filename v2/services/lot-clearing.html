Lot Clearing

Selective clearing of building envelope and driveway with mature shade trees preserved.

Building on a wooded lot in the Panhandle? We clear the building envelope, driveway, and yard areas while preserving the mature shade trees that make wooded lots worth buying.

What's included

  • Selective clearing of building envelope and driveway
  • Preservation of designated shade trees
  • Stump grinding within the pad area
  • Coordination with builder and site engineer

Frequently asked

Can I keep most trees and still build?

Often yes. Talk to your builder about root zone protection during construction.

How soon can I build after?

Most pads are ready for site work the next day. Stump-ground areas may need settling.

Do you grade?

Rough grade and surface prep. Final pad grading is your site contractor.
