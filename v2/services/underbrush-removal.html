Underbrush Removal

Remove palmetto, briar, and invasive understory — leave every tree you want.

A lot of Panhandle properties are beautiful under all that understory. Underbrush removal opens up the woods without taking down a single tree you want to keep.

What's included

  • Removal of palmetto, gallberry, yaupon, smilax
  • Careful work around mature pines and hardwoods
  • Mulch layer left in place to suppress regrowth
  • Hand finish around trunks and protected features

Frequently asked

Will you damage the trees you leave?

No — the mulcher head only contacts what we direct it at, and we hand-finish near trunks.

How is this different from bush hogging?

Bush hogs choke on anything woody. A forestry mulcher grinds standing palmetto and small trees into mulch.

Can you remove invasives like Chinese tallow?

Yes — combined with a follow-up herbicide pass on stumps it's one of the most effective initial treatments.
