Why mulching

One machine. One pass. No burn pile.

Compared to dozing and burning, forestry mulching is faster, cleaner, and far easier on your land.

  • No burning — no smoke, no county burn-ban headaches.
  • No hauling — material stays on site as mulch.
  • Protects soil — tracked machines spread weight, mulch prevents erosion.
  • Slows regrowth — the mulch mat shades out seedlings.
