Quotes and estimates

Quotes we provide are good for 30 days unless otherwise noted. Final pricing may adjust if the scope of work changes during the project; any change will be discussed with you before additional work begins.

Property access and safety

It is the property owner's responsibility to identify and disclose underground utilities, septic systems, irrigation lines, and any other hazards before work begins. We use marker flags and call Sunshine 811 where appropriate.

Permitting

Local and state permits — particularly for wetlands, conservation easements, or environmentally sensitive lands — are the responsibility of the landowner. We are happy to provide documentation needed to support a permit application.

Payment

Standard terms are 50% on scheduling and 50% on completion, unless otherwise agreed in writing. We accept check, ACH, and major credit cards (3% surcharge on cards).

Content on this site is provided for general information only and does not constitute a guarantee of pricing, timing, or outcome on any specific project.
