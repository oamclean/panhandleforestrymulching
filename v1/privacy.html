Privacy Policy

How we handle the information you share with us.

Last updated: April 2026

What we collect

When you contact us or submit a quote request, we collect your name, contact information, and the details of the property and service you're asking about. We do not use third-party advertising trackers on this site.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your inquiry, prepare a quote, schedule and perform work, and follow up on completed jobs. We do not sell or share your information with marketers.

How we store it

Inquiries and project records are kept in our business systems for the duration of the engagement and as required for tax, insurance, and warranty purposes — typically up to seven years.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses a small number of cookies for basic functionality. We may use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how visitors find our site, with IP addresses anonymized.

Your rights

You can request a copy of the information we have about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing info@panhandleforestrymulching.com.
