About Panhandle Forestry Mulching

A small, owner-operated team clearing land the right way across Northwest Florida.

We started Panhandle Forestry Mulching because we kept seeing the same thing on properties across the region: beautiful land lost to palmetto, briar, and invasive growth — and owners told the only options were bulldozers or burn piles. There's a better way, and we've built our whole business around it.

What we do differently

  • Fixed-price quotes. We walk your property, scope the work, and put it in writing. No hourly creep.
  • Selective clearing. We flag the trees you want to keep before any machine starts. You stay in control of the final look.
  • Low-impact equipment. Rubber-tracked carriers spread weight evenly so we don't rut your soil — especially important on Panhandle sand.
  • Owner on every job. The person who quotes the job is the person running the machine.

Our equipment

We run purpose-built forestry mulchers with carbide-tooth drums capable of grinding standing trees up to 8" in diameter in a single pass. Smaller hand crews handle finish work around fences, sheds, and tight tree islands.

Licensed, insured, and local

We carry full general liability and equipment insurance, and we're happy to provide a current certificate before any project begins. We live and work in the Panhandle — when there's a question about your job, we're a phone call away, not a call center away.

Counties we work in

Gadsden, Leon, Liberty, Jackson, Calhoun, and Washington. If you're outside that footprint, ask anyway — we travel for the right project.
