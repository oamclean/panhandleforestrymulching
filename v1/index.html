Why mulching

One machine. One pass. No burn pile.

Compared to dozing and burning, forestry mulching is faster, cleaner, and far easier on your property.

No burning

No smoke complaints, no county burn-ban headaches, no fire risk to your treeline.

No hauling

Material stays on site as mulch — saving dump fees and truck traffic on your road.

Protects soil

Tracked machines spread weight; the mulch layer prevents erosion on Panhandle sand.

Slows regrowth

The mulch mat shades out seedlings, giving you more time between maintenance passes.
