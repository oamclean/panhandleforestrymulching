Land Clearing Across the Florida Panhandle

Full lot clearing for homesites, pasture, food plots, and commercial pads — done quickly and cleanly.

Need a piece of land turned from overgrowth to usable in a hurry? We handle the full clearing process: forestry mulching for the brush and small trees, controlled removal of larger trees if needed, and optional stump grinding to prep for a build.

What's included

  • Mulching of all brush and small/medium trees
  • Selective removal of larger trees on request
  • Optional stump grinding and root rake
  • Cleanup of debris into windrows or even spread
  • Final grade-ready surface where requested

When this service makes sense

  • Building a homesite, barn, shop, or driveway
  • Opening pasture, paddocks, or food plots
  • Preparing a commercial pad or staging area
  • Recovering long-neglected acreage

How we approach the job

We start with a walkthrough and a fixed-price written quote. We coordinate with your surveyor, builder, or engineer if there is one. On large jobs we phase the work so you can see progress and adjust.

Frequently asked questions

Do you pull stumps?

Yes, when needed. For pasture or recreational land, leaving stumps below mulch is often fine. For building pads we grind or pull stumps as part of the scope.

Can you clear in wet conditions?

We can work damp ground but not saturated ground — it damages the soil and leaves ruts. After heavy rain we typically wait a few days before resuming.

Do you do permitting?

For wetlands or environmentally sensitive areas, permitting is the landowner's responsibility, but we are happy to point you to the right contacts and provide documentation needed for permits.
