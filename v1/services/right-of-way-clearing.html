Right-of-Way & Fence Line Clearing

Clean clearing for utility corridors, fence lines, driveways, and property lines — minimal disturbance to either side.

Whether you're clearing a new fence line, opening a private driveway, or maintaining a utility easement, we can grind a precise corridor without tearing up the surrounding land. Mulchers leave clean edges and a flat finish that's ready for fence, gate, or pipe.

What's included

  • Corridor widths from 8 ft to 30+ ft
  • Clean, finished edge along property lines
  • Mulch left in place or removed on request
  • Coordination with surveyors and fence contractors

When this service makes sense

  • Installing or replacing fencing
  • Opening a driveway to a homesite or hunting camp
  • Clearing utility or pipeline easements
  • Maintaining an existing right-of-way that has grown in

How we approach the job

We work to a flagged or surveyed line so the corridor lands exactly where it should. We pay particular attention to the edge condition — sloppy edges are the giveaway of cheap clearing work and we don't leave them.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a survey first?

For property-line work we strongly recommend it. We'll work to whatever you flag, so accuracy of the flags determines accuracy of the line.

Can you clear under power lines?

Yes, up to the height clearance our equipment safely allows. For taller standing trees under lines we coordinate with a tree service.

Will my fencer be able to work right after you?

Yes — most fence contractors love working a freshly mulched line because they can see the ground and don't have to dodge stumps.
