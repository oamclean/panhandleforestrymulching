Hurricane & Storm Debris Cleanup

Fast, on-site mulching of downed trees and storm debris across the Panhandle — no hauling required.

After a hurricane or major storm, downed pines and broken limbs can make a property unusable for months. We mulch storm debris in place — so you don't have to wait on dumpsters, haulers, or burn-ban lifts. Priority response for residents in the path of named storms.

What's included

  • On-site mulching of downed trees, limbs, and brush
  • Selective takedown of dangerous leaners (with arborist coordination)
  • Driveway and access road clearing
  • Defensible space restoration around structures

When this service makes sense

  • Hurricane recovery on rural and wooded properties
  • Properties with a burn ban or no safe burn area
  • Owners who can't access dumpster or hauler service
  • Properties needing fast access restoration

How we approach the job

During named-storm seasons we prioritize cleanup calls from existing customers and properties with safety access issues. We coordinate with insurance adjusters when documentation is needed.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with insurance?

We can provide itemized scopes and photos suitable for insurance claims. Payment is between you and your carrier — we invoice you directly.

How fast can you respond?

Within the Panhandle during normal weeks: usually 1–3 business days. After a major storm we schedule based on access and safety.

Can you take down standing damaged trees?

Small to mid-size leaners yes. For tall standing damaged trees near structures we coordinate with a licensed arborist or tree service.
