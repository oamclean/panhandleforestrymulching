Residential & Commercial Lot Clearing

Lot preparation for new builds — selective, clean, and sized to the parcel.

Building on a wooded lot in the Panhandle? We clear the building envelope, driveway, and yard areas while preserving the mature shade trees that make wooded lots worth buying in the first place. The result is a clean, level, build-ready site that still looks like the woods you bought.

What's included

  • Selective clearing of building envelope and driveway
  • Preservation of designated shade trees
  • Stump grinding within the pad area
  • Mulch evenly spread or windrowed for removal
  • Coordination with builder and site engineer

When this service makes sense

  • New construction on wooded lots
  • Adding an outbuilding or pool to an existing property
  • Subdivision lot prep
  • Pre-listing cleanup to make a wooded parcel show better

How we approach the job

We walk the lot with you and (where possible) the builder before starting. We mark every tree that stays. We clear in stages so you can change your mind on a tree if needed — once it's mulch, that decision is final.

Frequently asked questions

Can I keep most of the trees and still build?

Often yes. We can clear a tight building envelope and driveway while leaving the rest of the lot intact. Talk to your builder about root zone protection during construction.

How soon after clearing can I build?

Most pads are ready for site work the day after we finish. Stump-ground areas may need a few weeks of settling and a top-off of fill, depending on density.

Do you grade?

We do rough grade and surface prep. Final building-pad grading is typically handled by your site contractor with our coordination.
